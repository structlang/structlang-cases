# 🧠 StructLang · The Cognitive Operating System for Decision & Understanding

> **From intuition to structure. From experience to algorithm. From human cognition to AI reasoning.**  
> StructLang transforms how decisions are seen, analyzed, and computed.

---

## 🚀 Overview

**StructLang** is an open-source _cognitive structure language_ —  
a unified framework for making **intent, judgment, and interaction** explicit, symbolic, and computable.

It provides a new foundation for **human–AI co-reasoning** by revealing the deep structure behind how intentions shape perception and decisions.

### Core Goals

- **Make cognition visible** — turn intuition into explicit structure
    
- **Make decisions computable** — formalize judgment and interaction
    
- **Make reasoning collaborative** — bridge human and machine logic
    

StructLang spans **cognitive science, systems theory, social dynamics, and artificial intelligence**, forming the world’s first _computable decision structure language_.

## 🧩 Core Components

### 1. **Theoretical Framework**

A 35-chapter architecture with three recursive levels:

|Layer|Focus|Description|
|---|---|---|
|**Foundational**|Intent Space & Subject–Object Structure|How meaning is formed through compression|
|**Structural**|A-Q-T Model & 64 Interaction States|The “Periodic Table” of human decisions|
|**Dynamic**|Jump & Evolution Mechanisms|Predicting structural transitions|
|**Meta-Reflection**|Frame Relativism|Understanding bias, limits, and context|
|**Applied**|AI, Management, Psychology, Education|Real-world case studies and models|

> Goal: compress complexity into actionable, measurable cognitive structures.

---

### 2. **Unified Symbol System v5.0**

A minimal syntax for mapping cognition into symbolic form.

|Symbol|Meaning|
|---|---|
|`@intent`|Marks the driving purpose or direction|
|`I(t,b,r)`|Intent Vector (Time, Benefit, Risk)|
|`<>`|Subject–Object relation|
|`[T–T]`|Time window & stability|
|`=>`|Jump or transition marker|

Principles: **clarity > elegance**, **bias explicit**, **symbols as tools, not beliefs**.

---

### 3. **Practical Frameworks**

Validated across multiple domains:

- **Cognitive Psychology:** experience debugging, anxiety structure mapping
    
- **Management & Negotiation:** 64-state interaction diagnosis, power anchoring
    
- **AI Explainability:** symbolic-neural hybrid reasoning engines
