## ⚠️ Theory's Limitations & Boundaries

### What It Cannot Do

```
❌ Cannot solve all problems
   └─ Only effective within instrumental rationality framework
   └─ Cannot answer "should" value judgments
   └─ Cannot replace ethics and meaning pursuit

❌ Cannot predict everything
   └─ Acknowledges three meta-constraints (limited info/processing/verification)
   └─ Can only increase probability, not guarantee accuracy
   └─ Emergent phenomena and black swans unpredictable

❌ Not applicable to all situations
   └─ Clear theoretical boundaries (Ch. 33)
   └─ Framework-incompatible scenarios exist
   └─ Cultural differences may cause failure

❌ Cannot replace human care
   └─ Efficiency ≠ Meaning
   └─ Instrumental rationality has blind spots
   └─ Human dignity > Efficiency
```

### Usage Warnings

```
⚠️ Beware over-simplification
   └─ Compression inevitably loses detail
   └─ +- symbols cannot exhaust human complexity
   └─ Maintain sensitivity to nuance

⚠️ Beware instrumental rationality expansion
   └─ Not all relationships should be gamified
   └─ Maintain sensitivity to meaning/dignity/emotion
   └─ Intimate relationships unsuitable for excessive analysis

⚠️ Beware prediction overconfidence
   └─ Structural analysis ≠ Inevitable prediction
   └─ Always preserve uncertainty space
   └─ Prediction is probabilistic, not deterministic

⚠️ Beware ethical numbness
   └─ Can do ≠ Should do
   └─ Success ≠ Legitimate
   └─ Effectiveness ≠ Morality
```

### Perspectives That Need Supplementing

```
+ Existentialism: Meaning and absurdity, transcending instrumental rationality
+ Phenomenology: Direct experience of lifeworld
+ Ethics: Foundation of "should" and value judgment
+ Aesthetics: Dimension of beauty and art
+ Spiritual dimension: Pursuit beyond rationality
+ Relational ontology: Being as relation
```

  └─ From intuition to algorithm, from experience to science

## 💡 Six Usage Principles

1. **Understand foundations before tools**
    
    - Concepts → Cases → Tools → Practice
    - Don't skip theory and go straight to tools
2. **Practice in real scenarios**
    
    - Don't just theorize
    - Find 1-2 scenarios daily for annotation and analysis
3. **Build personal structural lexicon**
    
    - Record your 64-pattern case encounters
    - Accumulate your experiential pattern library
4. **Maintain critical thinking**
    
    - Don't blindly apply
    - Use theory when effective, abandon when not
5. **Combine with other perspectives**
    
    - Avoid single-framework thinking
    - Instrumental rationality + Humanistic care
6. **Always remember: Humans are the end**
    
    - Efficiency serves meaning
    - Tools serve humanity
    - Success ≠ Legitimacy
