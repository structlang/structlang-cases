## Section 1: Deep Reasons for Structural Similarity

### 1.1 A Shocking Phenomenon

Returning to the fake dog refund case, a shocking phenomenon is:

Me × Merchant: (+++) × (-+-)  
Me × Manager: (+++) × (-+-)

Two completely different people (one is a merchant, one is a civil servant), when facing me, present exactly the same structural configuration.

This is not coincidence.

### 1.2 Three Reasons for Similarity

**Reason 1: Consistency of My Strategy**

Facing merchant:

- My state: +++ (active, have evidence, sincere)
- My strategy: Rights protection, reasoning, pressure

Facing manager:

- My state: +++ (active, have complaint rights, sincere)
- My strategy: Complaint, reasoning, pressure

I used the same "operation mode" to handle two relationships  
→ My state naturally identical in both relationships

**Reason 2: Consistency of Their Response Patterns**

Merchant's response logic:

- Have capability to refund (+), but don't want to bear cost
- Delay when possible (-), attitude perfunctory (-)
- This is "delay strategy"

Manager's response logic:

- Have authority to handle (+), but don't want trouble
- Delay when possible (-), attitude perfunctory (-)
- Also "delay strategy"

Although their identities differ, they used the same coping strategy  
→ State naturally identical in relationships

**Reason 3: Isomorphism of Game Structure**

Two relationships essentially same type of game: "Rights defender vs Responsible party"

- Rights defender: wants to solve problem, has certain bargaining chips
- Responsible party: can solve problem, but doesn't want to pay cost
- Game nature: delay-push game

Same game type → produces same structural pattern  
Like different chess games → may form same board structure

### 1.3 General Pattern of Structural Similarity

When subject A faces multiple objects B1, B2, B3..., if satisfying:

1. A uses same strategy for all objects
2. All objects belong to same type (have same interest structure)
3. Game contexts similar

Then:  
A×B1, A×B2, A×B3... will present same structural pattern

This is "structural replication" phenomenon.

**Example Verification: Manager and Multiple Subordinates**

Scenario: Department manager managing 5 subordinates

Manager's management style:

- Strict requirements (+++)
- Uses same management method

5 subordinates' reactions:

- Subordinate 1: Superficial cooperation, actually passive (-+-)
- Subordinate 2: Superficial cooperation, actually passive (-+-)
- Subordinate 3: Superficial cooperation, actually passive (-+-)
- Subordinate 4: Genuinely cooperative (+++)
- Subordinate 5: Superficial cooperation, actually passive (-+-)

In 5 relationships, 4 are +++×-+-, 1 is +++×+++

Reasons:

- Manager's strategy consistent
- Most subordinates have same coping pattern (passive response)
- Only subordinate 4 is exception (proactive personality)

**Structural similarity is not accidental, but systematic.**

## Section 3: Leverage Points in Networks

### 3.1 Identifying Leverage Points

Structural transmissibility reveals a powerful strategy: introduce external force, utilize transmission effects.

**What is a leverage point?**

Leverage point = Key node that can trigger cascade effects

Characteristics:

1. Has strong influence on target object
2. I can relatively easily influence it
3. Its state change will transmit to target

In fake dog case:

- Target: Change merchant's state
- Leverage point: Mayor's hotline/regulatory manager
- Transmission path: Mayor → Manager → Merchant

### 3.2 Method for Finding Leverage Points

**Step 1: Map relationship network**

```
Mayor's hotline
   ↓
Regulatory department manager
   ↓
Me ←————→ Merchant
```

**Step 2: Assess influence**

Who has influence on merchant?

Option 1: Me (direct path)

- Influence: 2/5 (just a consumer)

- Influence: 4/5 (has enforcement power)
- Problem: Manager also uncooperative

Option 3: Mayor's hotline (second-level indirect path)

- Influence on manager: 5/5 (superior supervision)

Can I activate this leverage point?

- → Feasibility: High

Result: Indirectly achieve goal

## Section 4: Triple Locking Mechanism

Why is leap so certain in fake dog refund case? Answer: **Triple locking mechanism**.

### 4.1 Origin Locking: Inviolable Baseline

**What is origin locking?**

Origin is reference baseline defined by intention. But not all origins have same weight.

Weak origin (low weight):

- "I hope you cooperate"
- Other party can choose: cooperate or not cooperate
- No coercion
- Weight: 1-2

Medium origin (medium weight):

- "Contract stipulates you should cooperate"
- Has certain binding force
- Violation has cost, but not catastrophic
- Weight: 3-4

Strong origin (high weight):

- "Law requires you must cooperate"
- Inviolable
- Violation has severe consequences
- Weight: 5

**Origin Locking in Fake Dog Case**

Merchant's origin locked to "legal compliance":

Initial state:

- Merchant's origin: Profit maximization
- Don't refund if possible, delay when possible
- This is negotiable origin (weight 2-3)

After I introduce mayor's hotline:

- Merchant's origin forcibly switched: Legal compliance
- Consumer Rights Protection Law clearly stipulates
- Industrial and commercial department has enforcement power
- Consequences of violation: Fine, license revocation
- This is inviolable origin (weight 5)

Key transformation: From "delay when possible" game → to "must comply" execution

Manager's origin locked to "superior order":

Initial state:

- Manager's origin: Avoid trouble
- Delay when possible, don't want trouble
- This is his personal preference (weight 2)

After mayor's hotline intervenes:

- Manager's origin forcibly switched: Superior order
- Mayor's hotline requires deadline handling
- Not handling will be held accountable
- Consequences: Affects evaluation, career
- This is inviolable origin (weight 5)

Key transformation: From "personal preference" → to "organizational order"

**Three Elements of Origin Locking**

Element 1: Externality

- Effective origin locking often comes from external
- "Law stipulates you must..." (external system requirement, strong binding force)
- Not "I think you should..." (personal expectation, weak binding force)

Element 2: Coercion

- Origin must have coercive enforcement mechanism
- "If not cooperate, you'll be fined/fired/sued" (severe consequences, unbearable)
- Not "If not cooperate, I'll be unhappy" (consequences not severe, bearable)

Element 3: Non-negotiability

- Origin must be "yes/no question," not "choice question"
- "You must comply" (no bargaining room)
- Not "You'd better cooperate" (can bargain)

**How to Achieve Origin Locking**

Strategy 1: Cite external authority

- Weak expression: "I hope you refund" (your personal demand, I can refuse)
- Strong expression: "According to Consumer Protection Law Article X, you must refund" (legal requirement, I cannot refuse)

Strategy 2: Clarify consequences

- Vague consequences: "If you don't handle, I'll be very angry" (consequences unclear)
- Clear consequences: "If not handled by Friday, I'll call mayor's hotline, you'll be held accountable" (consequences clear and severe)

Strategy 3: Eliminate escape routes

- Leave escape route: "Let's discuss" (implies negotiable)
- No escape route: "This is not discussion, this is legal requirement" (no negotiation space)

### 4.2 Dimension Locking: Clear and High-Weight Meaning

Origin locking provides baseline, but not enough. Must give A/Q/T clear and high-weight meanings under this baseline.

**What is dimension locking?**

Dimension vague (unlocked):

- "Hope you handle it"
- Handle what? To what extent?
- Large interpretation space

Dimension clear (locked):

- "Must complete full refund before Friday"
- Action (A): Refund
- Standard (Q): Full amount
- Time (T): Before Friday
- No interpretation space

**Dimension Locking in Fake Dog Case**

Dimension locking for manager:

Mayor's hotline requirements:

Action dimension (A):

- Clear requirement: Immediately investigate, urge merchant to handle
- Not: Can delay, casually look
- Weight: Must do, not doing will be held accountable

Quality dimension (Q):

- Clear standard: Ensure consumer gets refund
- Not: Best effort, do what can be done
- Weight: Must achieve result

aTtitude dimension (T):

- Clear expectation: Take seriously, make it priority
- Not: Go through motions, perfunctory
- Weight: Must emphasize

All three dimensions clearly locked, no ambiguous space.

Dimension locking for merchant:

Manager's requirements:

Action dimension (A):

- Clear requirement: Immediate refund, handle same day
- Not: Consider it, talk in few days
- Weight: Execute immediately

Quality dimension (Q):

- Clear standard: Full refund, no discount
- Not: Partial refund, give vouchers
- Weight: Must meet standard

aTtitude dimension (T):

- Clear expectation: Actively contact consumer, attitude sincere
- Not: Wait for consumer to come, attitude perfunctory
- Weight: Must be genuine

Similarly, all three dimensions clearly locked.

**Three Values of Dimension Locking**

Value 1: Eliminate operation space

When unlocked:

- Instruction: "Handle this complaint"
- Other party understands: Casually deal with it/delay few days/partially solve
- Large operation space

When locked:

- Instruction: "Complete full refund before Friday"
- Other party understands: Can only be full refund before Friday
- Operation space compressed to zero

Value 2: Establish clear verification standard

When unlocked:

- "Did you handle it?" "I handled it, I called" "But not resolved" "I tried my best"
- Continuous disputes

When locked:

- "Refunded on Friday?" "Yes" / "No"
- Clear yes/no, no disputes

Value 3: Strengthen responsibility attribution

When unlocked:

- When problem occurs can shift responsibility
- "I thought you meant..." "My understanding was..." "Nobody told me to do this..."

When locked:

- When problem occurs cannot shift responsibility
- "Requirement very clear: Full refund before Friday" "Did you do it?" "If not, it's your responsibility"

**How to Achieve Dimension Locking**

Method 1: Use quantitative indicators

- Vague expression: "Handle ASAP" (what is "ASAP"?)
- Quantitative expression: "Complete before Friday 5:00 PM" (time point clear)

Method 2: Use operational definitions

- Vague expression: "Attitude should be good" (what is "good attitude"?)
- Operational definition: "Actively contact customer, reply within 24 hours, speak politely" (observable, verifiable)

Method 3: Use checklists

- Vague expression: "Do this project well" (what is "well"?)
- Checklist: "Complete following 5 items: 1. Submit proposal by Monday 2. Complete draft by Wednesday..." (each item checkable)

### 4.3 Time Window Locking: Must Decide Immediately

Origin and dimensions both locked, but if other party can still delay, leap won't occur. Third locking: Time window.

**What is time window locking?**

Time open (unlocked):

- "Handle it whenever convenient"
- Can delay indefinitely
- Leap may never occur

Time locked:

- "Must complete before Friday, otherwise bear consequences"
- Delay space compressed
- Must decide and act within window

**Time Window in Fake Dog Case**

Mayor's hotline supervision cycle:

Time setting:

- After mayor's hotline accepts case, set 7-day feedback period
- Must provide handling result within 7 days
- Will follow up after 7 days
- If not handled, will escalate accountability

Time locking effect:

- Manager faces: Countdown pressure, clear deadline, consequence threat
- Cannot delay, must act immediately

Manager's time requirement for merchant:

Time transmission:

- Manager to merchant: "You must handle today, I report to superior tomorrow"
- Time window: 24 hours
- Checkpoint: Tomorrow's report
- Consequence: Not handling will be reported to higher level
- Merchant cannot delay

**Three Levels of Time Locking**

Level 1: Set clear deadline

Weak time constraint:

- "Handle ASAP" "Do when free" "Complete early"
- No clear time point, can delay indefinitely

Strong time constraint:

- "Before Friday 5:00 PM" "Before noon tomorrow" "Before work today"
- Clear time point, delay space clear

Level 2: Establish inspection mechanism

No inspection:

- "Complete before Friday" (but nobody checks)
- Even with deadline, will delay
- "Nobody cares anyway"

Has inspection:

- "Friday 5:00 PM I'll check progress"
- "Tomorrow morning must report to boss"
- "Monday project review"
- Must complete before checkpoint

Level 3: Clarify overtime consequences

No consequences:

- "Complete before Friday" (but nothing happens if not completed)
- Deadline meaningless

Has consequences:

- "If not completed before Friday, project cancelled"
- "Overtime one day, penalty 1000 yuan"
- "Delay will affect annual evaluation"
- Must complete on time

**How to Achieve Time Window Locking**

Technique 1: Countdown visualization

- Constantly remind remaining time
- "3 days left" "1 day left" "Today is deadline"
- Create time urgency

Technique 2: Phased checkpoints

- Don't just have one final deadline
- Set multiple intermediate checkpoints
- "Monday check progress, Wednesday check draft, Friday final delivery"
- Each checkpoint is a small deadline

Technique 3: Escalating consequence mechanism

- Slight delay: Small penalty
- Serious delay: Large penalty
- "Overtime 1 day penalty 100, overtime 3 days penalty 500, overtime 7 days project cancelled"
- More delay, higher cost

### 4.4 Synergistic Effect of Triple Locking

Triple locking is not independent, but synergistic:

**Origin locking** → Determine "must do what"  
**Dimension locking** → Clarify "specific standard to achieve"  
**Time locking** → Force "must finish by when"

All three indispensable:

Only origin locking:

- "You must solve this problem" (but didn't say to what extent, when to complete)
- Other party can be perfunctory, delay indefinitely

Only dimension locking:

- "You must achieve full refund" (but didn't say why must do, when to complete)
- Other party can refuse, or delay indefinitely

Only time locking:

- "Handle before Friday" (but didn't say to what extent, why must do)
- Other party can be perfunctory

**Triple locking → Leap certainty**

Origin locking (weight 5) + Dimension locking (no operation space) + Time locking (no delay space)  
= Leap certainty approaches 100%

This is why prediction so accurate in fake dog refund case.
